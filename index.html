My Magic Words

English for kids aged 5-9, starting from zero

Help us test a kids' English game

Your child walks into a world, hears a word and taps the right thing: "Find the red apple!" Every word is spoken, so no reading is needed, and a short picture game at the start sets the level, even for a child who knows no English yet.

  • 🌍 Over 20 worlds: a farm, a supermarket, a kitchen, a school, a fun park, a safari, dinosaurs, space, a shopping mall and more
  • 🗣️ Words, colours, numbers, letters and short sentences, at three levels that grow with your child
  • 🛡️ No ads, no chat, plays offline
  • 🎁 Free to try: many worlds are free in the test

How you can help most

  • Keep it installed for at least 2 weeks and let your child play a few minutes every day or two. Google only lets a new app launch after real families have used it for 14 days.
  • Tell us what you think: in the app, tap I'm a parent on the start screen and fill in Tell us what you think. What did your child love? What was confusing?
  • The app updates itself through Play every few days with your ideas in it.
