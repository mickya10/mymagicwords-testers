Thanks for helping us! Installing takes a minute: two steps, using the same Google account that is signed in on your phone.

1
Join the testers group On the page that opens, tap Join group. The group never sends email. Join the group
2
Install from Google Play Tap Become a tester, then Install (or "Download it on Google Play"). Install from Google Play
If Play says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

What to test: play with your child for about 10 minutes, try the Kitchen and the Supermarket, and tell us what was confusing and what they liked. Some worlds are still being built, and the app updates itself through Play.
